Limits include the quiet zone. Rows × columns; taller codes carry a longer checksum. “Random pass” is the share of random cell patterns a reader would take for a code of this length and shape.

Printing it

  1. Use two filaments with strong contrast. Pairs close in tone, such as white on pink, may not read.
  2. Keep at least the M/2 quiet zone clear on all four sides. The backing already has it.
  3. Two dark areas that touch only at a corner are separate closed shells; some slicers may report this as a warning.

Print accuracy, reading distance and whether the minimum quiet zone is enough have not been measured across printers, materials and phones. Print one and test it before you print a hundred.
